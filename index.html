Coach Payroll

Monthly salary calculation based on scheduled hours, attendance, approved extra hours and approved shift covers.

Current payroll rules: Minimum attendance is 70%. If attendance falls below the minimum, the system recommends an attendance adjustment equal to the percentage-point shortfall, capped at 30% of basic salary. Attendance of 90%–94.99% earns a 2.5% bonus and 95%+ earns a 5% bonus. Approved overtime and shift-cover hours are paid at 1.5× the calculated hourly rate. Short-notice covers add Rs. 500 each, and 3 or more approved covers in the month add a Rs. 1,000 team support bonus. All rates remain editable in Supabase.
